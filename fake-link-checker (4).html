Common scams

KYC / bank block
“Account blocked, update KYC now.” Banks never ask this by link.
OTP theft
Anyone asking for your OTP is a fraudster. No exceptions.
Lottery / cashback
Prizes you never entered for are bait for your details.
Courier fee
Fake delivery texts with a small “redelivery fee” link.
UPI refund
You never need to enter your PIN to receive money.

Educational tool: a low-risk result is never a guarantee of safety.

Been Scammed?

  1. Stop all contact. Do not click or reply further.
  2. Call your bank or UPI app right away to block cards and payments.
  3. Call the cyber fraud helpline 1930 as soon as possible.
  4. Report the incident at cybercrime.gov.in.
  5. Keep screenshots, phone numbers and links as evidence.
